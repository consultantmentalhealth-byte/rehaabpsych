    Client Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

    Last updated: March 2026

    As a registered Consultant Psychologist, client confidentiality is a non-negotiable ethical priority. All information shared during individual or relationship counseling sessions, in intake forms, or via correspondence is strictly protected.

    What We Protect

    • Your personal identity, contact details, and session notes.
    • Online tele-therapy calls are conducted over secure, end-to-end encrypted video channels.
    • No session content is recorded without prior written consent.
    • We do not sell, share, or disclose client lists or records to third parties.

    Ethical Limits of Confidentiality

    In accordance with standard psychological ethical guidelines and legal frameworks, confidentiality may only be broken under the following extreme circumstances: (1) Credible threat of immediate physical harm to yourself or an identifiable other person, or (2) A direct legal court order. Wherever ethically possible, any required action is discussed with the client first.

    Terms of Service & Scope of Practice

    Last updated: March 2026

    These terms govern the psychological counseling and consultation services provided by Rehab Ali (Consultant Psychologist).

    Scope of Psychological Practice

    Counseling services provide psychological assessment, psycho-education, coping tools, and psychotherapeutic guidance. They do not constitute emergency psychiatric intervention or inpatient care. As a psychologist, I do not prescribe medical drugs; referrals to consulting psychiatrists are provided when appropriate.

    100% Online Tele-Counseling Services

    All sessions are conducted exclusively online via secure, encrypted video conferencing (Google Meet / Zoom) for adults (18+) across Pakistan and international jurisdictions. Clients are responsible for ensuring a private, quiet room and a stable internet connection.

    Appointment & Cancellation Policy

    Last updated: March 2026

    Because session slots are reserved exclusively for you, consistent attendance and clear notice allow us to respect both your time and the needs of other clients awaiting support.

    24-Hour Notice Policy

    You may reschedule or cancel your confirmed appointment with at least 24 hours advance notice at no charge. Notice can be provided via email or WhatsApp message.

    Late cancellations (under 24 hours) or missed sessions without prior notice may incur the standard session fee, as that dedicated time cannot be offered to another individual in need of care.

    If you arrive late for your session, we will still honor the remaining time of your scheduled appointment, but cannot extend past the reserved slot to prevent delays for subsequent appointments.
